# Contributing to the Multi-Phase Technical Methodology
Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Multi-Phase Technical Methodology for U.S. highway infrastructure.
Scholarly, technical, and professional contributions are welcome from transportation engineers, researchers, asset-management professionals, maintenance and reliability professionals, public-agency practitioners, and others with relevant expertise.
The purpose of these contributions is to support the continued evaluation, refinement, and practical development of the methodology.
# How to Contribute
### 1. Participate in Discussions
Use GitHub Discussions for broader technical and professional dialogue, including:
-	 Questions about the methodology; 
-  Technical or professional perspectives; 
-  Methodological limitations or assumptions; 
-  Implementation challenges;
-  Alternative approaches;
-  Validation considerations; and 
-  Relevant research, standards, or agency practices. 
You may respond to one or more methodology components that align with your expertise.
### 2. Submit a Technical Issue
Use GitHub Issues when you identify a specific technical concern, methodological limitation, implementation challenge, or actionable recommendation.
When submitting an Issue, please identify the relevant methodology component and, where applicable, explain:

- 	The technical observation or concern.
-   Why it is important.
- 	Its potential technical or practical implications. 
- 	A recommended improvement or alternative approach 
-   The professional, technical, or scholarly basis for the recommendation. 
### 3. Recommend Supporting Resources
Contributors may recommend relevant:

-  Peer-reviewed research;
-  Engineering standards or specifications; 
-  Transportation-agency guidance; 
-  Publicly available datasets; 
-  Technical reports; 
-  Analytical or decision-support methods; and 
-  Documented implementation examples. 
When possible, please briefly explain how the recommended resource relates to the methodology.
## Methodology Components
Contributions are particularly welcome regarding:
1.	Data-Driven Inspection and Condition Monitoring 
2.	Predictive Deterioration Modeling and Risk-Based Prioritization 
3.	Standardized Lean Maintenance 
4.	DOT Asset Management Integration 
5.	Continuous Performance Monitoring and Multi-State Application 
## Contribution Principles
Constructive and critical perspectives are encouraged. Contributors do not need to agree with the proposed methodology. Contributions should be technically relevant, sufficiently specific to permit meaningful evaluation, and supported by technical reasoning, evidence, or professional experience where appropriate.
Feedback identifying limitations, challenging assumptions, or proposing alternative approaches is welcomed as part of the methodology's continued development.
## Attribution and Disclaimer
Contributions represent the individual perspectives of contributors.Participation does not constitute formal academic peer review, co-authorship, certification of the methodology, institutional endorsement, or endorsement by a contributor's employer or professional organization.
Substantive contributions may inform subsequent revisions, validation activities, documentation, research, or further development of the methodology.
Thank you for contributing your expertise and helping to strengthen this work.
